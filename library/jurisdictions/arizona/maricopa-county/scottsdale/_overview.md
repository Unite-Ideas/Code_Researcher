# Scottsdale, AZ — adopted codes

- **Building code**: 2021 International Building Code (IBC), with local amendments, adopted
  by Ordinance No. 4550 / Resolution No. 12498, effective January 1, 2023. Codified at
  Scottsdale Revised Code (SRC) Chapter 31, Article I, Div. 1, Sec. 31-1.
  Source: https://www.scottsdaleaz.gov/docs/default-source/scottsdaleaz/codes---ordinances/building-codes/2021-international-building-code-(ibc)-amendments.pdf
- **Existing buildings / change of occupancy**: 2021 International Existing Building Code
  (IEBC), Article VIII of SRC Ch. 31, adopted per Resolution No. 12504. SRC Sec. 101.4.7
  routes repair, alteration, change of occupancy, addition, and relocation of existing
  buildings to the IEBC. Change-of-occupancy mechanics are in **IEBC Chapter 10** (Sections
  1001-1012, specifically §§1011.4-1011.6 for the actual egress/height-area/fire-protection
  consequences of a reclassification).
- **Plumbing code**: 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC), Article IV of SRC Ch. 31,
  adopted under a **separate resolution from the building code — Resolution No. 12501**
  (the IBC amendment ordinance is Resolution No. 12498; do not conflate the two when citing
  either). Scottsdale's Chapter 4 amendments (Sec. 31-83) touch only §410.1.1 (water-bottle
  filling stations), §421.4.3 (shower trench drains), and §424.1.1 (waterless urinals) —
  **§405.3.2 (lavatory-same-room-as-water-closet rule) and Table 403.1 (fixture counts) are
  not locally amended.**
- Arizona has no single mandatory statewide IBC edition for commercial buildings (ARS
  §9-802 lets municipalities adopt independently) — always confirm the *locally* adopted
  edition rather than assuming a state default.
- Scottsdale's local amendment ordinance amends Chapters 1, 4, 9, 11, 12, 16, 18, 21, 23,
  24, 25, 31 and the appendices. **Chapters 10 (Means of Egress) and 17 (Special
  Inspections) are not locally amended**; Chapter 16's amendment (Sec. 31-24) touches only
  §1612.3 (flood hazard areas) — §1613 (seismic design, including nonstructural components)
  is untouched. Unmodified 2021 IBC text governs all of these.
- **Permit revisions**: No standalone Scottsdale "field revision" policy document was found
  despite thorough searching. The operative standard is the **unamended 2021 IBC §107.4**
  ("Amended Construction Documents"): any change during construction not in compliance with
  approved documents must be resubmitted as amended construction documents. Scope/extent of
  the change (not convenience) is what should decide minor-revision vs. full-resubmittal
  treatment, at the Building Official's discretion. §107.4's text is verified directly
  against ICC's own rendered page (see Ground rules in SKILL.md for the technique) — no
  Scottsdale-specific amendment exists for it.
- **Zoning / parking**: governed separately from the building code, at Scottsdale Revised
  Code Appendix B (Basic Zoning Ordinance), Article IX, §§9.100-9.110 — a different legal
  instrument than SRC Chapter 31. Table 9.103.A sets the ratio for "Churches and places of
  worship": 1 space per 4 fixed seats (or 1 per 30 SF of sanctuary with no fixed seating),
  plus 1 space per 300 SF of classroom/meeting area on top of that. §9.107 ("Remote
  parking") is the mechanism for counting off-site/shared spaces toward this requirement —
  it requires a recorded agreement (Zoning Administrator + City Attorney approved, filed
  with the Maricopa County Recorder, minimum 5-year term) and the remote lot must be within
  600 feet of the served property, reachable by a safe continuous pedestrian route. A
  Parking Master Plan (§9.104.F) is the tool for a multi-phase/multi-permit project like
  this one to demonstrate parking adequacy per phase without treating each permit as a
  legally separate site.

## Key sections for repeat reference

| Topic | Section | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Change of occupancy | IEBC Ch. 10, §§1011.4-1011.6 | Governs B -> A-3 and similar reclassifications |
| Plumbing fixture counts | IPC Table 403.1 | Places of worship row; verify exact ratios against licensed source |
| Lavatory location relative to water closet | IPC §405.3.2 | "Public"/"employee" toilet rooms require the lavatory in the same room; "private" (single-fixture, restricted-access) rooms may not -- genuinely fact-dependent, not a bright line |
| Sprinkler trigger, Group A | IBC §903.2.1.3 | Fire area > 12,000 SF, or occupant load >= 300, or A occupancy not at level of exit discharge -- this project's main auditorium (1,462 seats) clears this by a wide margin |
| Fire alarm trigger, Group A | IBC §907.2.1 / §907.5.2.2 | Manual alarm at occupant load >= 300; emergency voice/alarm communication system at >= 1,000 -- both triggered here |
| Egress door types | IBC §1010.1.2 | Only side-hinged swinging, pivoted, or balanced doors qualify as an egress door (plus 9 narrow exceptions) -- overhead/coiling/roll-up doors are never a qualifying egress door type, verified directly against ICC's own page |
| Egress door swing direction | IBC §1010.1.2.1 | Swing in direction of travel where room/area occupant load >= 50; unchanged since 2018 IBC, not locally amended |
| Access-controlled egress doors | IBC §1010.2.10 (2021 numbering) | Sensor unlock, fail-safe on power loss, marked push-button override; renumbered again in later editions -- always confirm against the edition actually in force |
| Locking arrangements in educational occupancies | IBC §1010.2.8 (2021 numbering) | Governs lockdown-capable doors in classroom/childcare areas |
| Amended construction documents | IBC §107.4 | Governs when a field change requires formal resubmittal; verified directly against ICC's own page |
| Nonstructural component design (rigging, suspended AVL/LED) | IBC §1613.1 -> ASCE 7 Ch. 13 | Suspended equipment over an occupied Risk Category III assembly space (occupant load 300+, per IBC Table 1604.5) needs an engineered seismic/gravity design, not just an attachment-hardware cut sheet |
| Special inspections for seismic/designated systems | IBC Ch. 17, §1705.13 (esp. §1705.13.4, §1705.13.6) | May require manufacturer certification or special inspection of anchorage for suspended rigging/equipment, depending on Seismic Design Category |
| Deferred submittals | IBC §107.3.4, §107.3.4.1 | The correct process for AVL/rigging delegated design: identify on the EOR's drawings, submit sealed calcs, EOR reviews for general conformance, then building official approves before installation |
| Off-site/shared parking | SRC Appendix B (Zoning), §9.107 (Remote parking); §9.104.E/F (Shared Parking / Parking Master Plan) | Recorded agreement required before permit issuance -- an LOI or in-progress negotiation is not sufficient |

Arizona Office of the State Fire Marshal / DFFM administers a separate state fire code
baseline, but this applies mainly to state-owned facilities and school districts via ARS
§34-461 intergovernmental agreements — not the controlling code for a private house of
worship inside incorporated Scottsdale.

Arizona exempts a religious institution's nursery/kids-ministry program conducted in
conjunction with its services (no stated hours-per-week cap) from state child-care-facility
licensing under **A.R.S. §36-884(2)** — so Arizona Department of Health Services childcare
plumbing/ratio rules (A.A.C. Title 9, Ch. 5) do not apply to a weekend-only program, only to
a facility that adds standalone weekday daycare hours.
