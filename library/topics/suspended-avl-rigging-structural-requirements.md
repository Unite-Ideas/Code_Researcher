---
topic: Structural design and inspection requirements for suspended AVL/theatrical rigging
last_verified: 2026-09-15
---

Recurring question for any large assembly space (worship auditorium, theater, arena) with
suspended AVL/theatrical rigging, LED walls, speaker clusters, or truss attached to the
roof/ceiling structure.

**The base rule (2021 IBC, unless locally amended -- check the specific jurisdiction):**

- **IBC §1613.1** requires nonstructural components permanently attached to a structure to
  be designed to resist seismic effects per **ASCE 7 Chapter 13** -- this covers rigging,
  LED walls, and suspended equipment, not just the primary building structure.
- **IBC Table 1604.5** assigns Risk Category III to an assembly space where more than 300
  people congregate in one area. That raises the Component Importance Factor (Ip) under
  ASCE 7 §13.1.3 and can trigger the manufacturer-certification requirement in ASCE 7
  §13.2.2 for "designated seismic systems."
- **IBC Chapter 17, §1705.13** (especially §1705.13.4 "Designated seismic systems" and
  §1705.13.6 "MEP components") can require special inspection of the anchorage, depending
  on Seismic Design Category.
- **A generic manufacturer attachment/hardware cut sheet (e.g., a beam-clamp catalog page)
  does not, by itself, satisfy this.** The code requires an actual engineered design
  addressing the specific installed weight, eccentricity, and attachment location --
  practically, this means calculations sealed by a licensed engineer.
- The correct process is a **deferred submittal under IBC §107.3.4 / §107.3.4.1**: the
  engineer of record identifies the rigging/AVL package as deferred on the approved
  drawings, the specialty contractor's engineer submits sealed calculations, the EOR
  reviews for general conformance, and the building official approves before installation.

**A jurisdiction that scrutinizes this closely (e.g., asking for full calculations rather
than accepting attachment details alone) is very likely just enforcing the base IBC as
written, not applying an undocumented local rule** -- confirm by checking whether that
jurisdiction's own amendment ordinance actually touches IBC Chapter 16 (§1613) or Chapter
17 (§1705.13) before assuming a local-specific requirement exists.
