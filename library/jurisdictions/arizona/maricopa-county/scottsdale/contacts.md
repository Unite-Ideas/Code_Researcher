# Scottsdale / Maricopa County — agency contacts

Accumulated across projects. Add to this list rather than replacing it.

## Building permits / plan review / inspections

- **City of Scottsdale Building Safety Division / One Stop Shop**
  Phone: (480) 312-2500
  Web: https://www.scottsdaleaz.gov/planning-development
  Handles: building permits, plan review, field inspections, permit revisions.

## Fire marshal / fire prevention

- **City of Scottsdale Fire Prevention** — coordinate through Building Safety plan review
  for TI projects. Direct line not yet confirmed in research; verify before relying on it.
  status: needs_verification

## Health department (food service)

- **Maricopa County Environmental Services Department (MCESD), Plan Review & Construction
  Program**
  Phone: (602) 506-6824
  Email: ENVPlanreview@maricopa.gov
  Web: https://esd.maricopa.gov
  Handles: retail/commercial food establishment plan review and permitting, countywide
  (including within incorporated Scottsdale).

## Planning & zoning

- **City of Scottsdale Planning & Development Services**
  Web: https://www.scottsdaleaz.gov/planning-development
  Same general department as Building Safety for most commercial TI purposes; verify
  whether a distinct zoning contact is needed for this project's specific parcel. Also the
  point of contact for a Remote Parking Agreement / Parking Master Plan (Zoning Ordinance
  Appendix B, Article IX) -- these need both this office's Zoning Administrator and the
  City Attorney's approval before a permit can be issued.

## Recording / legal instruments

- **Maricopa County Recorder's Office**
  Web: https://recorder.maricopa.gov
  Handles: recording a Remote Parking Agreement, Shared Parking Agreement, or Parking
  Master Plan agreement against the properties involved -- required before the City will
  issue a permit that relies on off-site/shared parking to meet the zoning requirement.

- **City of Scottsdale City Attorney's Office**
  Web: https://www.scottsdaleaz.gov/city-attorney
  Handles: approval of Remote Parking Agreements and Shared Parking Agreements alongside
  the Zoning Administrator (Zoning Ordinance §9.107, §9.104.E/F).
