## Maricopa County, Arizona

Maricopa County Environmental Services Department (MCESD) regulates all retail/commercial
food establishments countywide (including inside incorporated cities like Scottsdale) under
the **Maricopa County Environmental Health Code (MCEHC), Chapter VIII, Section 2**.

Adoption chain: MCEHC Ch. VIII Sec. 2, Reg. 1 adopts by reference **the FDA Food Code,
2017 Edition, effective October 6, 2021, and no future editions or amendments**, with three
narrow local amendments (omits §3-304.15; amends §3-201.11(B) and §8-301.11 — none relate
to handwashing). Source:
https://www.maricopa.gov/DocumentCenter/View/5477/Chapter-8-Section-2---Food-Establishments-PDF

Plans for any new food establishment or major alteration must be submitted to and approved
by MCESD before construction; no grandfather clause exists for new build-outs. Source:
https://www.maricopa.gov/DocumentCenter/View/5797/Minimum-Requirements-PDF

### Handwashing: sink vs. sanitizer (a recurring question)

- Employee handwashing procedure: FDA Food Code 2017 §2-301.12 (cleaning procedure) and
  §2-301.14 (when to wash).
- Physical handwashing sink requirement: §5-203.11 (numbers/capacities), §5-204.11
  (location — convenient to food-prep/dispensing areas), §5-205.11 (operation — accessible
  at all times, no other use).
- Hand sanitizer is only ever a supplement applied to hands **already washed at a sink**
  per §2-301.16(A)(3) — it is never a substitute for the sink.
- The only sink-substitution exception is §5-203.11(C): chemically treated towelettes (not
  sanitizer stations), and only where food exposure is limited and a sink is not
  conveniently available (mobile/temporary establishments, vending machine locations),
  with prior MCESD approval. A permanent café counter inside a finished tenant improvement
  does not qualify.
- MCESD's own plan-review guide classifies any bar/counter preparing espresso or steamed
  milk drinks (a TCS food) as an "Open Food Service and Beverage Establishment," which
  explicitly requires a hand sink "centrally located, visible, and directly accessible
  (approx. 25 feet) from all food and beverage workstations/areas, including all bars."

Bottom line for Maricopa County: expect a plan-review rejection if a hand sink is
omitted in favor of sanitizer alone for any on-site espresso/coffee prep counter.
