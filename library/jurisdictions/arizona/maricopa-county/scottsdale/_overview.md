# Scottsdale, AZ — adopted codes

- **Building code**: 2021 International Building Code (IBC), with local amendments, adopted
  by Ordinance No. 4550 / Resolution No. 12498, effective January 1, 2023. Codified at
  Scottsdale Revised Code (SRC) Chapter 31, Article I, Div. 1, Sec. 31-1.
  Source: https://www.scottsdaleaz.gov/docs/default-source/scottsdaleaz/codes---ordinances/building-codes/2021-international-building-code-(ibc)-amendments.pdf
- **Existing buildings / change of occupancy**: 2021 International Existing Building Code
  (IEBC), Article VIII of SRC Ch. 31, adopted per Resolution No. 12504. SRC Sec. 101.4.7
  routes repair, alteration, change of occupancy, addition, and relocation of existing
  buildings to the IEBC. Change-of-occupancy mechanics are in **IEBC Chapter 10** (Sections
  1001-1012).
- **Plumbing code**: 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC), Article IV of SRC Ch. 31.
  Fixture counts: IPC Table 403.1. Confirm exact current figures against a licensed
  ICC/UpCodes source before finalizing a stamped set — automated fetch of ICC's own pages
  was blocked (403) during research; figures were corroborated across independent
  secondary sources but not read verbatim from ICC directly.
- Arizona has no single mandatory statewide IBC edition for commercial buildings (ARS
  §9-802 lets municipalities adopt independently) — always confirm the *locally* adopted
  edition rather than assuming a state default.
- Scottsdale's local amendment ordinance amends Chapters 1, 4, 9, 11, 12, 16, 18, 21, 23,
  24, 25, 31 and the appendices. **Chapter 10 (Means of Egress) is not locally amended** —
  unmodified 2021 IBC text governs egress questions (door swing, access-controlled doors,
  classroom locking arrangements) as of last verification.
- **Permit revisions**: No standalone Scottsdale "field revision" policy document was found
  despite thorough searching. The operative standard is the **unamended 2021 IBC §107.4**
  ("Amended Construction Documents"): any change during construction not in compliance with
  approved documents must be resubmitted as amended construction documents. Scope/extent of
  the change (not convenience) is what should decide minor-revision vs. full-resubmittal
  treatment, at the Building Official's discretion. Confirm directly with Scottsdale
  Building Safety / One Stop Shop for any specific project.

## Key sections for repeat reference

| Topic | Section | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Change of occupancy | IEBC Ch. 10 (Secs. 1001-1012) | Governs B -> A-3 and similar reclassifications |
| Plumbing fixture counts | IPC Table 403.1 | Places of worship row; verify exact ratios against licensed source |
| Sprinkler trigger, Group A | IBC §903.2.1.3 | Fire area > 12,000 SF, or occupant load >= 300, or A occupancy not at level of exit discharge |
| Fire alarm trigger, Group A | IBC §907.2.1 | Manual alarm at occupant load >= 300; voice/alarm communication at >= 1,000 |
| Egress door swing direction | IBC §1010.1.2.1 | Swing in direction of travel where room/area occupant load >= 50; unchanged since 2018 IBC, not locally amended |
| Access-controlled egress doors | IBC §1010.2.10 (2021 numbering) | Sensor unlock, fail-safe on power loss, marked push-button override; renumbered again in later editions -- always confirm against the edition actually in force |
| Locking arrangements in educational occupancies | IBC §1010.2.8 (2021 numbering) | Governs lockdown-capable doors in classroom/childcare areas |
| Amended construction documents | IBC §107.4 | Governs when a field change requires formal resubmittal |

Arizona Office of the State Fire Marshal / DFFM administers a separate state fire code
baseline, but this applies mainly to state-owned facilities and school districts via ARS
§34-461 intergovernmental agreements — not the controlling code for a private house of
worship inside incorporated Scottsdale.
