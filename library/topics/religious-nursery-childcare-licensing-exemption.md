## Arizona

**A.R.S. §36-884(2)** exempts from all Title 36, Chapter 7.1 child-care-facility licensing:
"a religious institution conducting a nursery in conjunction with its religious services or
conducting parent-supervised occasional drop-in care." Source: https://www.azleg.gov/ars/36/00884.htm

Unlike the separate exemption for secular educational programs for ages 3-6 (§36-884(8),
capped at 2.25 hours/day, 3 days/week), the religious-nursery exemption carries **no stated
hours-per-week limit** as written. A weekend-only kids' ministry program run during
services fits this exemption. If a church later adds standalone weekday daycare hours not
tied to a religious service, that additional program may fall outside the exemption and
trigger Arizona Department of Health Services child-care licensing (A.A.C. Title 9, Ch. 5),
which carries its own, stricter plumbing/fixture-ratio rules distinct from the base IPC.

Always confirm the exemption still applies to the actual program as operated (frequency,
supervision model, whether it is tied to a religious service) before relying on it -- this
note establishes the citation and general shape of the exemption, not a case-specific
determination.
